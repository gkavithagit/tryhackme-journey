🎯 Purpose of the Room

The Careers in Cyber room introduced the different career opportunities available in the cybersecurity field and helped me understand the skills, responsibilities, and learning paths associated with different cybersecurity roles.

🧠 What I Learned

Through this room, I learned that cybersecurity is a broad field with different career paths rather than a single job role.

Some areas of cybersecurity include:

- Security Operations
- Defensive Security
- Offensive Security
- Digital Forensics
- Incident Response
- Security Engineering
- Governance, Risk and Compliance
- Threat Intelligence

🔍 Key Takeaways

1. Cybersecurity has multiple career paths

Different cybersecurity roles require different technical skills, responsibilities, and areas of specialization.

2. Practical skills are important

Cybersecurity is not only about learning theory. Hands-on practice with systems, networks, tools, and security scenarios is an important part of developing practical skills.

3. Continuous learning matters

Cybersecurity is a constantly changing field. Building a strong foundation and continuously practicing new concepts is important for developing a career in this field.

4. Different roles require different skills

Some roles focus more on defending systems, while others involve identifying vulnerabilities, investigating incidents, analyzing threats, or managing security risks.
